Checking

Ready means the lane, every surcharge and every charge the Incoterm puts on the customer is on the sheet, valid on the cargo ready date, in the quote currency, and the email gives one weight. Anything else is held back with the reason and the cells.

Chargeable weight is the higher of gross weight and volume ÷ 6,000 cm³ per kg, rounded up to the next 0.5 kg. When the next weight break gives a lower charge, that one is used and both cells are shown. The chargeable weight is then the break's weight, and surcharges on chargeable weight follow it.
